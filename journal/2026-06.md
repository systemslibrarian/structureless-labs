## 2026-06-10 update — atlas polish round + groundwork for SPEC-001

Incremental work the day after the operational-month-zero baseline.
None of the headline counts move (no new published concept; no new
finding; no new sl-kem decision); the day was about *how well* the
existing artifacts work and what the next move requires.

### What landed

- **LWE interactive — gold-standard rewrite.** Configurable
  Toy / Small / Medium presets (each with prime `q` so modular
  inverses are guaranteed); deterministic seed (mulberry32) exposed
  in the Export panel for offline reproduction; an attack-surface
  explorer that *literally executes* modular Gaussian elimination
  in the browser; per-row verification pills; per-cell `s′` vs. `s`
  diff highlighting; noise-distribution sparkbar with `Σ|eᵢ|`,
  mean and mean/q ratios; hash-routable preset
  (`#/lwe?preset=medium`); WAI-ARIA arrow-key navigation on the
  preset radiogroup. Recorded under
  [D-0001 (sl-atlas)](../sl-atlas/decisions/D-0001.md).
- **Encoding interactive — second live demo.** Bitwise modular
  encoding on `Z_q`; controls for `q`, `η`, and the encoded bit;
  decoding-region visualization on the `Z_q` ring; live decoder
  outcome (✓ correct / ✗ failure); exact CBD-PMF sparkbar with the
  failure mass colored; live `δ = Pr[|e| > τ]` computation.
  Honest non-claim block: Reed-Muller and Barnes-Wall are not
  simulated. Recorded under
  [D-0002 (sl-atlas)](../sl-atlas/decisions/D-0002.md).
- **`sl-atlas/decisions/` directory inaugurated.** Implementation-
  level decisions for the atlas now live here; method-level ones
  remain at `sl-researchkit/decisions/`. The boundary is the
  directory.
- **CI hardened.** New
  [`scripts/check-lwe-math.mjs`](../scripts/check-lwe-math.mjs)
  invariant test runs both interactives' math: I1 (prime q whitelist),
  I2 (noise-off recovers s exactly with all m rows verifying), I3
  (noise-on returns wrong vector with only n forced rows verifying),
  I4 (CBD PMF sums to 1 for η ∈ [1,6]), I5 (CBD closed-form for
  η ∈ {1,2} matches textbook). Wired into `validate.yml`.
- **sl-researchkit reusability — STARTER.md.** A single-file
  bootstrap so an external researcher can adopt the method on a
  non-PQC project in under an hour. Nine numbered steps, copyable
  commands, the verbatim agent prompt that drives a six-perspective
  review. Linked from [`sl-researchkit/README.md`](../sl-researchkit/README.md).
- **Authoring brief for SPEC-001.** `sl-kem/spec/AUTHORING-BRIEF-SPEC-001.md`
  maps the open decisions D-0003 (sl-kem) deferred — D-0004
  (reference selection) through D-0009 (side-channel posture) — with
  a candidate-comparison table, persona-load anticipation, pre-merge
  checklist, and a six-persona review prompt template. No
  parameters fabricated; no persona votes invented. SPEC-000 cross-
  references it.
- **README + REPRODUCE refresh.** New "How a concept is born" ASCII
  diagram in [`README.md`](../README.md) visualizes the three-depth
  model and the Teacher gate's BLOCK path. [`REPRODUCE.md`](../REPRODUCE.md)
  gains a "Verify the LWE interactive" walkthrough.
- **New confidence-scored bet on the record.**
  [PREDICTION-003](../sl-kem/predictions/PREDICTION-003.md) — D-0004
  (sl-kem reference selection) will be authored, with all six-persona
