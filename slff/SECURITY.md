# Security Policy

## This is experimental research

Structureless Labs publishes **experimental** post-quantum cryptography research. With the
explicit exception of clearly-marked reviewed/standardized primitives used for hybrid
constructions, **nothing here is validated for production use.** For real-world protection
today, use standardized algorithms (e.g. ML-KEM, ML-DSA).

## We want you to break it

Reporting a weakness is the highest-value contribution you can make. File it in
`sl-attacklab` as a finding, or for sensitive coordinated disclosure, open a private
security advisory on the affected repo.

## What we promise
- Every confirmed break gets a decision record and a public write-up.
- Failed attacks are still valuable and still logged.
- We will never claim more than the evidence grade supports.
